About Taipei Food

Welcome to Taipei, a city famous for its delicious and affordable street food.

From busy night markets to small local shops, you can discover many different Taiwanese flavors.

Good food makes a good trip.

Must-Try Foods

🍚 BRAISED PORK RICE
A classic Taiwanese dish with savory braised pork over rice.

🧋 BUBBLE TEA
A classic Taiwanese drink with chewy pearls.

🥞 SCALLION PANCAKE
Crispy outside and soft inside.

🍜 BEEF NOODLES
A warm and flavorful Taiwanese classic.

Food Tips

BRING CASH
Many small food shops accept cash only.

TRY SMALL PORTIONS
This allows you to try more different foods.

GO EARLY
Popular food stalls can become very crowded.

SHARE WITH FRIENDS
Sharing food lets you taste more dishes.
